## ❓ Common Questions

### "Do I still need to click Save?"
Only if you want to close the form. Auto-save keeps running!

### "What if my internet is slow?"
Auto-save waits for the save to complete. You'll see "Saving..." longer.

### "Will it create many profiles?"
No! It creates one draft, then updates that same one.

### "Can I turn it off?"
Nope! It's a safety feature to protect your work.
