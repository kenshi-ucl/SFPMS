# ⚡ Auto-Save Quick Start Guide

## What's New? 💾

Your profile form now **automatically saves your work** as you type!

---

## How It Works (Simple Version)

1. **Start typing** in the profile form
2. **Wait 3 seconds** after you stop
3. **See "Saving..."** indicator appear
4. **Done!** Your work is saved to the database

---

## Key Features

### 🔄 Auto-Save
- Saves every **3 seconds** after you stop typing
- Works on **all fields** (name, address, education, etc.)
- **Silent** - doesn't interrupt your work

### 💾 Draft Persistence
- Close your browser anytime
- Come back later
- Your work is **automatically loaded**!

### ✅ Visual Feedback
- **"Saving..."** - Currently saving
- **"Last saved 2:45 PM"** - Successfully saved
- **Green checkmark** - Everything is safe

## Common Questions

### Q: Do I still need to click "Save as Draft"?
**A:** Only if you want to close the form. Auto-save keeps saving while you work!

### Q: What if I close my browser?
**A:** Your data is safe! Auto-save already saved it to the database.

### Q: Will it create multiple profiles?
**A:** No! It creates one draft, then updates that same draft.

### Q: How do I know it's saving?
**A:** Watch for the **"Saving..."** indicator at the bottom of the form.

### Q: Can I disable it?
**A:** Auto-save can't be disabled (it's a safety feature), but you can ignore it!

## Important Notes

### ⚠️ Auto-save only works when:
- ✅ You've entered at least your name
- ✅ You're in create or edit mode (not viewing)
- ✅ You wait 3 seconds after making changes

### ℹ️ Good to know:
- Auto-save keeps status as **"draft"**
- Only **"Submit for Review"** changes status to **"submitted"**
- You can still manually save anytime
- Auto-save happens in the background

## Troubleshooting

### Not Saving?
1. Check: Did you enter your name?
2. Wait: Full 3 seconds after last change
3. Look: See the "Saving..." indicator?
4. Check: Browser console for errors

### Still Having Issues?
1. **Hard refresh:** `Ctrl + Shift + R`
2. **Clear cache:** Browser settings
3. **Check network:** Is backend running?
